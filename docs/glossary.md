# Glossary

## Invitation

A single-use link, valid for at most seven days, that lets its holder request to join a Tenant. Its issuer can choose direct joining when they may approve Registration Requests for that Tenant. Direct joining is the default; Aidash rechecks the issuer's authority when the link is used and otherwise leaves the request pending. See [Invitations](authorization.md#tenancy-rules-tenant-creation-and-invitations).
