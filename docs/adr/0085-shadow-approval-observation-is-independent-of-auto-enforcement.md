# Shadow approval observation is independent of Auto enforcement

Decided on 2026-10-23 for #103 and #109. A Tenant may explicitly select an exact, Catalog-approved Shadow approval Decider to observe the approvals its people decide. Observation is independent of the person's Approval Mode and Rules and does not change, settle or withhold the protected call. Existing Auto enforcement, Tenant ceilings and designated-approver checks remain governed by ADR 0076 and ADR 0077.

Observation covers the supported approval kinds: Tool call, Outbound, Web search, Page reading and Generation. Reports keep kinds separate. Only a person's explicit approval or refusal is a calibration label. Rule- and mode-given approvals, Auto decisions, pending approvals, expiry, cancellation, revocation, missing evidence and unavailable Deciders are separate populations. A report never treats an unlabelled record as a denial or presents fixture agreement as observed human calibration. It never changes thresholds automatically: a threshold change creates a new immutable Decider version requiring approval.

The state boundary remains ADR 0077's: authorized human inputs, Agent instructions and prior calls without results, plus the pending action and its authoritative definition. An approved observer does not obtain permission to receive private content. Observation obeys current disclosure authority, durable attempt accounting and the selected version's call budget. Missing state or unavailable authority is an explicit unavailable observation, not guessed context or a grant.

An approval's existing authorized viewers may see its Shadow verdict, probabilities, version and Route. Tenant-level aggregates require the Tenant's administrative permission. Both projections exclude classification-state bodies, raw arguments, source contents and credentials, and never widen the underlying approval or Run visibility. Decision Evidence remains retained with its Run under the existing policy.

## Considered options

- Observe only calls reaching the Auto judge: rejected because Manual and explicit-ask decisions would be missing from the comparison population.
- Treat automated outcomes or expiry as human labels: rejected because agreement would measure the system against itself or infer a decision nobody made.
