# Preserve research evidence and explicitly authorize query disclosure

Aidash retains the source URL, title, retrieval time and bounded passages used as evidence under the associated Run's access and retention policy, rather than relying on later pages remaining unchanged or permanently archiving whole sites; provider selection must accommodate that durable history. A Run containing nonpublic or unclassified material requires an eligible person's approval of the exact outbound search query, while public-only research can proceed automatically, because model-based secret detection cannot establish permission to disclose information. Provider-side bounded operational retention of public queries is acceptable, but the applicable service terms must prohibit using submitted queries for model training; the initial deployment does not require Japan-only processing or zero retention.
